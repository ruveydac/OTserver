# Contributing & Code Ownership

We love community contributions. To help keep OTserver open source and
sustainable over the long term, contributors must sign the Contributor License
Agreement (CLA) before a pull request can be merged.

## Why do we require a CLA?

OTserver uses a dual-licensing model: AGPLv3 and commercial licensing. Revenue
from commercial enterprise licenses funds the full-time development,
maintenance, and security of the open-source codebase.

The open-source core is here to stay: the scanner, all detection needed to
find assets and device capabilities, and the base asset-management interface
features remain open source. Enterprise options may include optional
proprietary add-ons, such as SSO integrations or customized dashboards. Your
contributions strengthen the shared core that everyone can use and improve.

By signing the CLA:

- You retain ownership of your copyright.
- You grant us a license to distribute your contributions under both the AGPLv3
  open-source license and our commercial license.
- You help keep the project legally protected and sustainably maintained for
  everyone.

Signing the CLA is automated and takes about 30 seconds when you submit your
first pull request.
